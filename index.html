The Atelier

Born from shadow,
refined by time

Founded in the quietude of a centuries-old atelier, Noir Absolu emerged from a singular obsession: to capture the profound elegance of the unseen.

Every vessel is a testament to the slow art of traditional perfumery. We allow our extraits to macerate in darkness for no less than twelve moon cycles, ensuring a depth that unfolds only on the warmth of the skin.

In an age of speed, we choose the deliberate path. Our ingredients are ethically sourced from the fringes of the world, selected for their purity.

Scent Profile

An Olfactory Journey through Darkness and Light

I

Top Notes

Black Pepper &
Bergamot

A sharp, invigorating awakening. The spice of crushed black pepper meets the citrus brilliance of Calabrian bergamot, cutting through the shadows.

II

Heart Notes

Midnight Rose &
Saffron

The core reveals a velvet darkness. Intoxicating Bulgarian rose intertwined with the leathery warmth of rare saffron threads.

III

Base Notes

Oud Wood &
Smoked Amber

A lingering, resonant finish. The ancient depth of agarwood anchored by the smoldering resinous sweetness of amber and patchouli.
